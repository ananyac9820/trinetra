## Label statistics

### IMD category distribution

Reported as counts, not as a balanced set, because the imbalance is the reason
the classification heads use class-balanced focal loss and the reason macro-F1
and a full confusion matrix are reported instead of overall accuracy.

| Category | Meaning | Lower bound (kt, 3-min) | Fixes |
|---|---|---|---|
| D | Depression | 17 | 6,048 |
| DD | Deep Depression | 28 | 2,211 |
| CS | Cyclonic Storm | 34 | 1,893 |
| SCS | Severe Cyclonic Storm | 48 | 701 |
| VSCS | Very Severe Cyclonic Storm | 64 | 595 |
| ESCS | Extremely Severe Cyclonic Storm | 90 | 345 |
| SuCS | Super Cyclonic Storm | 120 | 46 |

The scale is stored as inclusive lower bounds rather than as the published
whole-knot ranges. Best-track values are not whole knots once a 1-minute wind has
been converted, so a range table silently drops every value that lands between
two bands.

### Rare events

| Label | Positives | Labelled fixes | Base rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| RI, 30 kt or more in 24 h | 321 | 9,263 | 3.47% |
| Re-intensification over land, 10 kt or more in 24 h while inland | 23 | 9,263 | 0.25% |

The RI base rate is why the head is scored with Brier Skill Score against
climatology, a reliability diagram, and POD / FAR / CSI at the operational
threshold, rather than with accuracy. The land re-intensification count is a
case-study basis. It is not enough for a calibrated probability and the model
card says so.

### Label noise

| | Value |
|---|---|
| Fixes analysed by both IMD and JTWC | 5,555 |
| Mean absolute IMD minus JTWC difference | 7.02 kt |
| Median absolute difference | 5.01 kt |

Computed after converting JTWC's 1-minute sustained wind to IMD's 3-minute
averaging period with the conventional 0.93 factor. Comparing the raw numbers
inflates the disagreement by roughly 7 percent of the wind speed.

This is the observational uncertainty of the labels. Published satellite
intensity estimation sits in the 8 to 10 kt RMSE band, which is the same order.
Any intensity RMSE this project reports below about 5 kt should be treated as a
leakage bug to find rather than a result to present.

### Regime label caveat

The four regime classes are weak labels. No published regime product exists for
this basin, so they are derived from geometry and intensity trend:

| Class | Rule | Fixes |
|---|---|---|
| `over_land` | `dist2land_km <= 0` | 2,636 |
| `post_landfall_remnant` | has already touched land, now back over water or inland | 1,383 |
| `sheared` | over ocean, below 34 kt or weakening by more than 5 kt in 12 h | 5,483 |
| `maritime_mature` | everything else | 2,491 |

`sheared` is the weakest of the four, because it is an intensity-trend proxy for
a condition that properly needs a shear field. When ERA5 credentials are
available, `harmonise/environment.py` replaces the proxy with a real
200 to 850 hPa shear threshold. Until then the class is a proxy and the methods
page labels it as one.

## Splits

Grouped by storm from the first experiment, never by fix. Leave-one-season-out is
the headline protocol. The split configuration is written to
`data/labels/splits.json` and published on `/methods`, so the reported numbers
can be reproduced rather than taken on trust.

Splitting by fix would leak, because consecutive 3-hourly fixes of the same storm
are near-duplicates. A model split that way scores well and has learned nothing.

## Known gaps

The independent-truth subset is specified and empty. NOAA SAR TC Wind carries an
estimated Vmax for tropical storms, which would be a non-Dvorak intensity truth
and the single most credibility-enhancing number available to this project. The
client is written in `ingest/sar.py`. It has never been run against the real
archive, so no independent-truth RMSE is reported anywhere, and the methods page
shows that row as absent rather than filling it in.

ATMS and altimetry are deliberately out. Passive microwave is already covered by
GMI, AMSR2 and SSMIS, and TCHP already carries the ocean-fuel concept.

Wind radii stay out of scope as a headline claim. R34, R50 and R64 exist in
IBTrACS under `USA_R34_*` and are parsed, but they are only reported on the
SAR-validated subset, which is currently empty.
